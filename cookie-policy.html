What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, remember your preferences, and provide information to the website operator.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies — temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.
  • First-party cookies — set by the website you are visiting (CryptoFXMiners).
  • Third-party cookies — set by other domains whose content appears on the website.
02

How We Use Cookies

CryptoFXMiners uses cookies and browser storage for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your login session so you remain signed in while using the platform.
  • To store authentication tokens securely in your browser.
  • To remember your currency and language preferences.
  • To protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks.
  • To keep the platform secure and functioning correctly.

We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking, or to build profiles for marketing purposes.

04

Third-Party Cookies

CryptoFXMiners integrates with the following third-party services that may set their own cookies or tokens:

  • Supabase — our backend and authentication provider. Supabase uses secure cookies to manage authenticated sessions. These are essential and cannot be disabled without breaking the platform.
  • Google Fonts — we load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set cookies or log requests. No personal data from our platform is shared with Google.

We do not integrate with Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics, advertising networks, or any other third-party tracking services.

05

Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use your browser's localStorage to store non-sensitive preference data such as your selected currency and sidebar state. This data:

  • Remains on your device until you clear your browser data.
  • Is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Contains no personal or financial information.
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.
06

Your Cookie Choices

You have the following options regarding cookies:

  • Accept all cookies — the platform functions fully with all features available.
  • Essential cookies only — you can disable functional/preference cookies, but some features like saved currency preferences will not work.
  • Block all cookies — if you block all cookies, you will not be able to log in to the platform as authentication requires session cookies.

Essential authentication cookies cannot be disabled without losing access to your account. They are strictly necessary for the platform to function securely.

07

Managing Cookies by Browser

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Here's how in major browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Opera: Settings → Advanced → Privacy & security → Site Settings → Cookies

Note that clearing cookies will log you out of CryptoFXMiners and reset your saved preferences.
